# Optional Demo Video Script — 58 Seconds

Status: production plan. Record only after T090 verifies the final candidate on the target device. The official limit is under one minute; judges are not required to watch beyond one minute.

## Capture prerequisites

- exact submitted app version running from the final public Reddit post;
- real mobile or desktop Reddit/Devvit footage, with at least one mobile segment;
- notifications and personal browser/account details hidden;
- a real populated creator card if it appears; otherwise use the documented no-card fallback;
- no copyrighted music or unlicensed marks beyond recorded app UI/assets.

## No-card fallback

If the final live candidate cannot produce truthful Route Passport values, do not mock or seed them for capture. Replace 47–55 seconds with Achievements plus a second Roadbook route and say: “Daily routes, achievements, and permanent community history make tomorrow worth checking.”

## Edit checklist

- [ ] Runtime is 58 seconds or less after upload processing.
- [ ] First five seconds show the hook, not setup screens.
- [ ] UI remains readable without relying on narration.
- [ ] Captions match the spoken script.
- [ ] No password, token, private subreddit URL, notification, or unrelated tab appears.
- [ ] Video host is YouTube, Vimeo, Facebook Video, or Youku and visibility is public.
- [ ] Final watched upload matches the submitted link and exact app version.
